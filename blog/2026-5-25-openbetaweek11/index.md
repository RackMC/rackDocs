It is now week 11 of the RackMC Open Beta and the start of week 3 of the Factions Season 0 playtest! 

One thing to note is that in order to appear on `/ftop`, your faction must have more than 0 power. Power is gained by playing on the server and lost through inactivity. This means if the members of your faction are inactive for too long, your power will drop to 0 and you will no longer be on `/ftop`.

Don't worry! You still have 14 days to maintain your faction's power and climb your way to the top of the leaderboard. Remember, the prizes of **the special tag for the top 3 factions** and **Netherite rank for the number 1 faction** are still up for grabs. 

Here is a list of what has happened over the past week, as well as any planned changes for this coming week:
