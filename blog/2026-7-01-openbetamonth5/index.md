It is now the fifth month of the RackMC Open Beta. Unfortunately, the month of June was a busy month for all of the admins and we weren't able to get much done. Don't worry though, we have not forgotten about it. 

That being said, I never got to make an announcement after the playtest was over. Despite the smaller player count, we still were able to get a lot of good data in terms of economy, kits, etc. which will allow us to make sure the server is balanced on launch. We still have some other decisions to make, such as what version we would use when we do an official season of Factions, 1.8 or the latest. If you have any thoughts or opinions on that or anything else regarding the server, open a ticket on [discord](https://discord.rackmc.net).

Here is a list of what has happened over the past month, as well as any planned changes for this coming month:

## Upcoming Changes
### Factions
- The Factions shop prices and economy balancing is still in the works
### Events
- We have begun to plan and work on a few event ideas, but no solid plans yet

## Bug Fixes
### Network
- You can now join all servers on the latest version of Minecraft (26.2)

## Known Issues
### Network
- Tags are currently equipped per server instead of globally
- The playercounts on the scoreboard don't always update correctly
### Lobby
- Hunger does not drain during duels
### Lands
- `/rtp` does not work (use `/wild` for now)
